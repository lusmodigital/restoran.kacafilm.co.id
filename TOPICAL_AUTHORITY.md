## Evidence gates

- Glazing type, film compatibility, thermal stress, breakage, safety/security retention, fire/egress, food-area suitability, and warranty require glass identification, manufacturer approval, tested assemblies, and current project rules.
- Heat, glare, UV, visible-light, energy, HVAC, temperature, fade, privacy, and payback claims require named metrics, baseline measurements, product data, and comparable post-installation evidence.
- Graphics, logos, photography, and patterns require rights and owner/landlord approval.
- Prices, schedules, life, cleaning intervals, and warranties require dated written evidence. Cracked, chipped, or moving glass requires isolation and competent assessment.
